About & Contact

Last updated: 27 November 2026

CompoundLab is a free, no-signup compound interest calculator built for clarity. It models how an initial investment grows with a chosen interest rate and compounding frequency, and lets you layer on the things that happen in real life: regular deposits, withdrawals, annual contribution increases and inflation-style withdrawal increases.

What it can do

  • Compound, daily, forex and simple interest modes
  • 8 currencies and 12 compounding frequencies (including custom)
  • Deposits and withdrawals at any frequency, made at the start or end of a period
  • Effective annual rate (APY), nominal vs effective rate, rate of return and time-to-double
  • Stacked growth chart, monthly and yearly breakdowns, and CSV export
  • Light and dark themes, works on phones, tablets and desktops

How it works

Everything runs in your browser with JavaScript — there is no server, no account and no data collection. The projection is simulated day by day, so contributions and withdrawals land exactly where you schedule them. Read the disclaimer for the assumptions behind the numbers.
