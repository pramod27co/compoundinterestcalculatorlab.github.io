What is compound interest?

Interest earned on both your original money and the interest it already earned — the snowball effect that accelerates long-term growth.

How to use this calculator

Enter your amount, rate, term and compounding frequency, then add deposits or withdrawals to model real saving or drawdown scenarios.

Why frequency matters

The more often interest compounds, the faster your balance grows — a small edge that compounds into a big gap over long horizons.
