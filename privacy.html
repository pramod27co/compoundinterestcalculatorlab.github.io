Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

CompoundLab ("we", "us") operates this compound interest calculator. This page explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the calculator runs entirely in your browser and we do not collect personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or payment details.
  • We do not have user accounts and we do not store your calculations on a server.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any personal information.

2. Local storage (your device only)

We store one small preference in your browser's local storage: your light/dark theme choice (compoundlab-theme). It never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

3. Calculations stay on your device

All numbers you enter are processed locally with JavaScript. Nothing you type is transmitted to us.

4. Third-party services

  • Fonts — Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this site. Google may receive your IP address when the font files are requested.
  • Advertising — if display ads are shown, they will be served by an advertising partner such as Google AdSense, which uses cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings, or learn more at Google's policy on technologies.
  • Analytics — if privacy-friendly traffic statistics are added later, they will be aggregated and non-identifying.

5. Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. Third parties (such as an advertising network) may set cookies as described above.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the site's functionality changes. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit the About & Contact page.
